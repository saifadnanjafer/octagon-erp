# CAP-07 — AI, People, and Vertical-Pack Review

This read-only review does not authorize AI to execute high-risk finance,
payroll, stock, security, legal, or quality actions. On disposable databases,
the Phase 02 security suite passed 24/24, including AI tool and service-identity
overreach, scoped/masked evidence views, approval concurrency, audit, and
provider-outage behavior. Authority coverage passed 9/9: every governed action
has audit/outbox/idempotency evidence and no authority captures the frozen
payroll, attendance, timesheet, or workshop execution zone.

`cutover_governance` is explicitly classified as a control-plane module with
no business facts; the coverage test now reflects that boundary. CAP-08 remains
a closure review, not a claim of browser acceptance.
