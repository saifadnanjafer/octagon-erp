## 1. Executive Summary
The **Budgeting, Forecasting, and Financial Planning** module establishes a governed platform foundation for managing cost center hierarchies, annual/quarterly fiscal budgets (`BDG-2026-XXXX`), budget line encumbrance holds & over-budget commitment guards, inter-line budget reallocations (`REAL-2026-XXXX`), rolling financial forecasts (`FCST-2026-XXXX`), and macroeconomic/strategic scenario parameters.

---

## 2. Implemented Components

### Database Schema (Migration 073)
- `database/migrations/073_budgeting_and_financial_planning.mjs`
- 8 Schema Entities:
  1. `cost_centers`: Cost center hierarchy codes, names, parent associations, and department managers.
  2. `fiscal_budgets`: Annual/quarterly budget headers (`BDG-2026-XXXX`), fiscal year, currency, total budgeted, committed, and actual amounts.
  3. `budget_lines`: Detailed line allocations per GL account, cost center, and monthly period.
  4. `financial_forecasts`: Rolling financial forecasts (`FCST-2026-XXXX`), scenario types (baseline, optimistic, conservative), and projected net income.
  5. `financial_forecast_lines`: Line item revenue/OPEX/CAPEX projections.
  6. `budget_reallocations`: Budget transfer tracking between lines (`REAL-2026-XXXX`), audit reasons, and CFO approval stamps.
  7. `budget_commitments`: Pre-encumbrance holds generated by purchase orders or contracts against budget lines.
  8. `financial_scenarios`: Growth rates, inflation expectations, and USD/IQD exchange rate parameters.

### Domain Service (`platform/domains/financial_planning/service.mjs`)
- `createCostCenter`: Cost center setup.
- `createFiscalBudget`: Fiscal budget initiation (`BDG-2026-XXXX`).
- `addBudgetLine`: Line allocation and total budget recalculation.
- `approveFiscalBudget`: Transitioning budget status to `active`.
- `commitBudgetAmount`: Encumbrance guard checking `budgeted - (committed + actual) >= amount`. Throws error on over-budget attempts.
- `reallocateBudget`: Inter-line budget reallocations (`REAL-2026-XXXX`), deducting from source line and adding to destination line.
- `createFinancialForecast`: Rolling forecast model creation (`FCST-2026-XXXX`).

### ActionExecutor & Permissions (`platform/domains/financial_planning/index.mjs`)
