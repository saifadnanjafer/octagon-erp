A **decentralized plastic recycling micro-factory** that:

1. Collects plastic waste locally

3. Transforms it into valuable products using multiple manufacturing methods

## ✍️ Final 3-Sentence Solution (Refined)

This project is about building a small-scale recycling system that turns plastic waste collected locally into useful products. We use simple processes like shredding, large-scale 3D printing, and plastic injection to make items such as furniture, construction components, and other durable products that can be used in everyday life.

The idea is to make better use of different types of plastic instead of letting them end up in landfills or the environment. By combining different production methods, we can turn waste into things like panels, interlocking blocks, and reusable parts while creating opportunities for local youth and waste collectors.

In the long term, this system can be expanded to produce larger construction elements and materials that help with climate adaptation, showing that plastic waste can become a valuable local resource.
