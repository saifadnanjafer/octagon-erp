The $4.2B Problem

Event planning in Iraq is broken. Here's why:

Fragmented Ecosystem

No centralized platform exists. Couples juggle 15+ vendors via WhatsApp, phone calls, and in-person visits. Coordination takes 200+ hours.

High Risk of Failure

Supplier cancellations ruin events. No backup systems. No verification. No escrow protection. Families lose money and memories.

Budget Chaos

No transparency in pricing. Hidden fees. No budget tracking. Average wedding overspend is 40% above initial budget.

Time Intensive

Planning a wedding takes 3-6 months of active coordination. Most couples have full-time jobs. Stress levels are extreme.

Zero Quality Assurance

No ratings system. No verified portfolios. No dispute resolution. You book blind and hope for the best.

Language & Tech Barriers

Existing tools are English-only and require technical literacy. Iraqi dialect and cultural nuances are completely ignored.
