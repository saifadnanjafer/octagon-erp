Octagon — System maintenance authorization required
النظام في وضع التشخيص فقط. قاعدة البيانات التشغيلية بحاجة إلى ترحيل معتمد من المالك. جميع شاشات العمل والعمليات معطّلة حتى يتم التفويض وإعادة تشغيل النظام.
Health-only mode. The operational database has pending migrations that require owner authorization. All business routes are closed. A clean restart is required after the migration is authorized and applied.
Automatic startup migrations are disabled. Migrating an operational database requires an explicit, owner-approved command with a verified backup and a completed staged rehearsal.
